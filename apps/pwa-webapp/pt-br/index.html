Explore Rianell

Symptom & vitals tracking

Log pain, fatigue, symptoms and vitals in a fast guided flow that turns into clear trends.

Mental-health check-ins

Private mood tracking and optional PHQ-9 / GAD-7 style check-ins that are never stored.

On-device AI insights

Weekly reviews and clinician briefs generated locally - your health data never leaves your device.

Anonymised community

Opt in to compare with others who share your condition and help people like you.

Condition guides

Learn what to track for your condition and how to prepare for appointments.

Private & open source

Encrypted, optional cloud sync. Open code. Export or delete everything at any time.

Free to start, private by design

The core app is free with no account required. Create an account only if you want encrypted sync across your devices. Your health data is encrypted, EU-hosted when synced, and never sold.

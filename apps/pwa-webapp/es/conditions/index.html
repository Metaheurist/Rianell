Capture symptoms, medications and lifestyle to correlate with flares and remission.

Turn tracking into better care

Consistent logging is only half the story. The goal is to walk into an appointment with a clear, honest picture of your recent weeks - not a vague memory. Rianell helps you:

  • Choose a small, sustainable set of things to track for your condition.
  • Watch trends instead of single readings, so noise doesn't mislead you.
  • Export a concise report and, optionally, an AI-drafted brief for your clinician.

These guides are general information, not medical advice. Your care team knows your situation best - use your logs to inform that conversation.

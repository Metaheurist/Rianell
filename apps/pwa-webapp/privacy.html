Privacy Policy

Rianell Health App - Last updated: 27 June 2026

1. Who we are

Rianell ("we", "our", "us") is a personal health-tracking application available at rianell.com. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Data we collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Account data - email address and password hash (via Supabase Auth).
  • Health logs - symptom scores, weight, heart rate, exercise entries, and notes you enter manually.
  • Connector data - if you connect Strava, Withings, or Google Sheets, we store OAuth access tokens (AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest) and any health metrics fetched through those integrations.
  • Device / usage data - anonymous crash reports and performance metrics only, never tied to your identity.

3. How we use your data

  • To store and display your health logs across devices (Cloud Sync).
  • To sync data from third-party services you explicitly authorise.
  • To improve app reliability and fix bugs.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for AI training without your explicit consent.

4. Data storage & security

All cloud data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database hosted in the EU (Ireland, eu-west-1). OAuth tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being written to the database. Row-Level Security (RLS) ensures each user can access only their own data.

5. Third-party integrations

Connecting an integration (Strava, Withings, Google Sheets) is optional and requires your explicit authorisation. We request only the minimum scopes needed. You can disconnect any integration at any time from Settings → Connectors.

6. Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Privacy → Delete Account, or by emailing us at privacy@rianell.com. Deletion is permanent and takes effect within 30 days.

7. Cookies & local storage

We use browser local storage to cache your health logs for offline use. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us at privacy@rianell.com to exercise any of these rights.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available at rianell.com/privacy.html. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact

Questions? Email privacy@rianell.com.

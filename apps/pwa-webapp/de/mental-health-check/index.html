Frequently asked questions

What are the PHQ-9 and GAD-7?

They are widely used self-report questionnaires about how often you have experienced certain feelings over the past two weeks. Rianell offers them as optional, private check-ins to help you notice changes over time. They are screening tools, not a diagnosis.

Does Rianell store or upload my answers?

No. Individual screening answers are processed on your device and are never written to logs, local databases, or the cloud.

Is a check-in a diagnosis?

No. A check-in can help you reflect and start a conversation with a qualified professional. If you may be at risk, contact a local health service or emergency line right away.

Do I need an account?

No. Mood tracking and check-ins are free and work without an account. You can optionally create one later for encrypted sync.

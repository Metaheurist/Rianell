Help others with your condition - and learn from them

Chronic illness can feel isolating. Rianell's opt-in community turns individual logs into anonymised, privacy-preserving insight, so you can see how people with the same condition are doing and contribute to knowledge that helps the next person.

How the community works

Anonymised by default

Shared insights use privacy-preserving aggregation, so no individual can be identified from the results.

Always opt-in

Nothing is shared unless you explicitly turn on data sharing. You can turn it off again at any time.

See people like you

Compare trends with others who share your condition and discover what tends to help.

Your contribution matters

When you choose to contribute, your anonymised patterns join a shared pool that reveals what people with your condition experience over time. It is a way to give back - to help researchers, clinicians and fellow patients understand conditions that are often under-studied.

  • Contribute anonymised data to help improve understanding of your condition.
  • Benefit from collective insight without exposing anyone's private records.
  • Stay in control: sharing is opt-in and reversible.

Community insights are informational and not a substitute for professional medical advice.

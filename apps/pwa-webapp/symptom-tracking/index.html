What you can track

Symptoms & pain

Rate pain and fatigue, mark symptom severity, and pinpoint pain regions on a body map.

Vitals

Weight, heart rate, blood pressure and blood glucose, with clean charts over time.

Lifestyle

Sleep, steps, hydration, exercise and food (including barcode scanning) in one place.

Medications & appointments

Keep medications and appointments alongside your logs so nothing gets lost.

Cycle tracking

Optional menstrual-cycle tracking that ties into how you feel day to day.

Notes

Add free-text notes to any entry to capture context a number can't.

From daily logs to real insight

Individual entries are useful, but the value comes from trends. Rianell turns your history into charts you can actually read, highlights what changed, and helps you prepare a concise summary to bring to appointments.

  • Spot which days, activities or treatments line up with better or worse symptoms.
  • Export a clean report to share with your GP, specialist or care team.
  • Keep everything in one private place instead of scattered notes and apps.

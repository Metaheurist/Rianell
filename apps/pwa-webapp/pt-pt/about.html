Rianell

Personal health & chronic condition tracker

Rianell is a free, open-source personal health application designed to help people living with chronic conditions track symptoms, monitor key health metrics, and prepare meaningful insights to share with their healthcare team.

What Rianell does

  • Daily health log - symptoms, pain scores, fatigue, mood, mobility, and more
  • Vitals tracking - weight, heart rate, blood pressure
  • Medication and appointment management
  • AI-assisted trend analysis and condition insights
  • Export reports to share with clinicians
  • Optional cloud sync across devices (end-to-end encrypted)
  • Connect fitness and health devices via Strava, Withings, and Google Sheets

Google Sheets integration

Users can optionally connect a Google Sheets spreadsheet to import existing health data or export their log for backup and analysis. The app accesses only the single spreadsheet the user explicitly provides. No other files or data are accessed.

Privacy & security

All cloud data is stored encrypted. OAuth tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Row-Level Security ensures each user can only access their own data. The app never sells or shares your data.

Open source

Rianell is fully open source. The source code is available on GitHub.

Everything in one place

One app to track, understand, and share your chronic condition

Rianell is a free, open-source personal health app. Log symptoms and vitals in seconds, spot trends with on-device AI, and prepare clear reports for your care team - all while your data stays encrypted and private.

Start free - no account needed Sign up to sync & help others

Symptom & vitals tracking

Log pain, fatigue, symptoms, weight, heart rate, blood pressure, sleep, steps, hydration and more in a fast guided flow.

Mental-health check-ins

Private mood tracking with optional PHQ-9 and GAD-7 style check-ins. Responses are never stored or uploaded.

On-device AI insights

Weekly reviews, trend explanations and clinician briefs generated locally on your device - no health data sent to a server.

Anonymised community

Opt in to compare with others who share your condition, and contribute anonymised data to help people like you.

Condition guides

Learn what to track for your condition and how to turn your logs into better conversations with clinicians.

Private & open source

Encrypted cloud sync is optional. The code is open. You can export or delete everything at any time.

Why people choose Rianell

Free forever

The core app is free with no account required. Sign up only if you want encrypted sync across devices.

Privacy-first

Health data is encrypted, EU-hosted when synced, and never sold. Screening answers never leave your device.

Works offline

Install it as an app on phone or desktop and keep logging even without a connection.

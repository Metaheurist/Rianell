Private by design

Most health apps send your data to a server for AI features. Rianell takes the opposite approach: the model runs in your browser or device, so the analysis happens where your data already lives. Nothing about your health is transmitted to run these features.

  • Runs on-device using efficient models, with graceful fallback on lower-powered hardware.
  • Works alongside encrypted, optional cloud sync - you choose what leaves your device.
  • Insight is guidance, not medical advice.

AI output is informational only and can be imperfect. Always confirm important decisions with a qualified healthcare professional.
